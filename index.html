Website Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website dev360.github.io (the "Website").

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information: usage data, browser type, device information, IP address, and any information you voluntarily provide through contact forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to improve our website, analyze traffic patterns, respond to your inquiries, and provide a better user experience.

4. Cookies

We may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and personalize content. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as analytics tools that collect anonymous usage data to help improve our website.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us if you wish to exercise these rights.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: ademthedeveloper@gmail.com

Game 1 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile game "Game 1" and describes how we handle your personal information within the app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect: device information, game progress, crash logs, and usage statistics. No personal identifiers such as name or phone number are collected without your consent.

3. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with our game. This data helps us improve the gaming experience and fix bugs.

4. Advertising

Our app may display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant advertisements based on your interests.

5. Data Storage

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. We do not store personal data on our servers unless required for gameplay.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries: ademthedeveloper@gmail.com
